# Owner review checklist

- Verify and confirm the correct academic record wording, including: "Second Class Honors" (site) vs "Excellency" (LinkedIn), and graduation July vs June 2022.
- Confirm the correct title and role wording: "Trainee" vs "Law Intern", and "Legal Specialist" vs "Senior Legal Specialist".
- Confirm whether the public court-representation claim is accurate or whether it should be restricted to the current role only.
- Verify the GRC certificate issuer and wording: "Al Yamamah University" vs "Marketing Club".
- Confirm the exact wording and validity of the phrase: "Professional Legal Accreditation, Saudi Arabian Bar Association, 2024".
- Confirm the correct Arabic spelling of his name and whether the site should list an openToRegion / work-outside-Saudi-Arabia option.
- Confirm whether the public site should solicit clients directly or remain recruiter-focused; this affects lawyer-advertising positioning.
- Check all public PDFs for personal identifiers, licence numbers, barcodes, signatures, national IDs, student IDs, or other sensitive data before publication.
- Confirm the live status of the domain mailbox and DNS records, including MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and whether the domain address is a working mailbox or just a forwarding alias.
- Confirm the Web3Forms access key and whether the redirect should remain on the custom domain.
- Confirm Search Console and Bing verification ownership and whether verification files or domain ownership records are already in place.
- Review the final contact CTA and public legal-service positioning before launch.
